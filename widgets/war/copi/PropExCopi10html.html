Exercise 1 (of 1).

There are a number of well-known theorems which express equivalences between formulas. Experienced logicians know these off by heart. You have already derived many of them. Be sure you can derive the rest

Absorption

1) ∴ (F∨(F.G))≡F
2) ∴ (F.(F∨G))≡F
3) ∴ (F⊃(F⊃G))≡(F⊃G)

Association

4) ∴ F∨(G∨H)≡(F∨G)∨H
5) ∴ F.(G.H)≡(F.G).H

Commutation

6) ∴ F∨G≡G∨F
7) ∴ F.G≡G.F
8) ∴ (F≡G)≡(G≡F)
9) ∴ F⊃(G⊃H)≡G⊃(F⊃H)

Contraposition

10) ∴(F⊃G)≡(∼G⊃∼F)

De Morgan's Laws
11) ∴ ∼(F.G)≡∼F∨∼G
12) ∴ ∼(F∨G)≡∼F.∼G

Distribution

13) ∴ F.(G∨H)≡(F.G)∨(F.H)
14) ∴ F∨(G.H)≡(F∨G).(F∨H)

Double Negation
15) ∴ F≡∼∼F

Equivalence
16) ∴ (F≡G)≡(F⊃G).(G⊃F)
17) ∴ (F≡G)≡(F.G)∨(∼F.∼G)


Excluded middle

18) ∴ F∨∼F

Exportation

19) ∴ (F⊃(G⊃H))≡((F.G)⊃H)

20) ∴ (F.F)≡F 21) ∴ (F∨F)≡F

Implication
